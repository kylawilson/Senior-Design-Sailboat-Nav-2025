**Hardware Installation and Setup:**

The Triton is composed of three individual sensor modules that contain stereo cameras and other sensors to relay data to the app. These modules are placed within semi-permanent mounts that the user must install at specific locations on their ship prior to first use. (see Figure 1)

*Initial Installation*
- Using a flathead screwdriver, attach and tighten the hose clamps of the central module to the railing at the bow of the boat, ensuring the camera is facing away from your ship.  (Position 1 in Figure 1)
- Wrap the velcro on the sides of the module around a portion of the railing so that it is taut and attach the remaining slack to the velcro attached to the module. For best security, wrap it around the pole at least two times.  
- Using the flathead screwdriver, attach and tighten the hose clamps of the side modules to lifelines(side railings) at or close to the widest part of your boat. (Left module Position 2 and Right module Position 3 in Figure 1)
- Make sure to push down on both side modules to ensure the suction cups on the bottom of the modules are attached.
- Tighten or loosen the attached velcro so that it is snug against the lifeline pole and attach the remaining slack to itself.

*Before Every Use* 
- Connect the battery pack to each of the modules to power on the devices.
- Place each module in their respective mounted frames, making sure the modules are fully sealed to the frames.
- Place the buckle on the module on the latch attached to the frame and turn the latch so that it is in a horizontal position.  
- For best readings, raise the telescoping pole attached to the anemometer, wind vane, and GPS antenna by twisting the metal adjuster to the left to loosen and raise the pole then twisting the adjuster to the right to tighten and lock the position of the pole in place.
- Launch the iOS application and connect to the modules
